# Completed Journal card art brief

This brief records the original scene requirements for the newly active cards. All 52 card masters and all 18 Lesson masters were generated and reviewed in the Journal copperplate style. The final bundle is `/home/dev/workspace_artifacts/reviews/journal-art-20260927/`.

## Shared composition and production

- Keep the useful mechanism large and recognizable at 256×384, with one foreground subject and an uncluttered background.
- Follow `art-grammar.txt`: warm copperplate engraving, controlled amber and muted mineral color, tactile materials, directional light, and deliberate negative space for code-rendered text.
- Exclude humans, humanoids, body parts, humanlike spirits, character silhouettes, mannequins, generated lettering, UI, frames, and ornamental borders. Do not show a vanilla Nether portal, rectangular obsidian frame, or purple portal plane anywhere in Font art.
- Preserve the pack’s actual objects and sequence. A visual may suggest a later step in the distance, while the foreground shows the specific trigger. Avoid fake recipes, invented item shapes, and inferred machine layouts.

## New-card scenes

### 1. Choose Your Return Point (`choose_return_point`)

Trigger: You arrived at the starting world as a spectator.

Composition: A quiet high view over three possible camp sites, one marked by a small warm beacon; no character silhouettes or interface.

Teaching emphasis: Your first chosen personal return point matters before permanent play begins. Beds later will not move it. Scout a safe place, choose your return point, then finalize your class.

### 2. Retired: Find Recipes in EMI (`find_recipes_in_emi`)

Trigger: You have had a moment to get your bearings.

Composition: An empty inventory worktable with three unlabelled material fragments connected by fine copper lines to a ghostly finished tool; no letters, numbers, or interface.

Teaching emphasis: EMI shows recipes and uses for the items around you. It is the quickest way to investigate a new material. Open your inventory, focus an item, and inspect its recipe and uses in EMI.

### 3. Make a First Hand Axe (`first_hand_axe`)

Trigger: You spent 30 active seconds in the world.

Composition: Loose rock, flint, bone, a slender handle and hanging roots arranged around an unfinished hand axe on a rough timber stump; no hands or UI.

Teaching emphasis: A primitive hand axe gets the first wood before a full tool station exists. Rock, flint, or bone can form the head; hanging roots offer an underground handle route. Use EMI to assemble a hand axe, then gather the wood for Tinkers stations.

### 4. Start With Tinkers Tools (`start_tinkers_tools`)

Trigger: You made a primitive hand axe.

Composition: Primitive axe beside a timber Part Builder, separate pick head and handle, and a completed Tinkers pickaxe under soft workshop light.

Teaching emphasis: The hand axe starts wood gathering, while a Part Builder and Tinker Station assemble your first durable mining tool. Use the wood to make those stations, build tool parts, and assemble a pickaxe.

### 6. Underground Pressure Builds (`underground_pressure_builds`)

Trigger: The underground pressure began to build.

Composition: Cave cross-section with subtle dust and fissures gathering below a clear sunlit surface path, the earliest hint of danger.

Teaching emphasis: Time spent in caves increases pressure before any encounter warning sounds. Returning to the surface lets it ease. Keep an exit route in mind and retreat to daylight when the pressure rises.

### 8. Hotstone Is Dangerous Ground (`hotstone_dangerous_ground`)

Trigger: You approached exposed Hotstone.

Composition: Glowing hotstone patches breaking through a dark path, heat shimmer and a safe pale route curving around them.

Teaching emphasis: Hotstone is hazardous underfoot, even when it looks like ordinary terrain from a distance. Mark a safe path around it before stepping closer.

### 10. Bring Planting Stock Home (`bring_planting_stock_home`)

Trigger: Wild Overworld vegetables left no planting seed.

Composition: Wild overworld vegetable plot yielding roots without seed, contrasted with a luminous Bumblezone seed carried to tended farmland; no hands.

Teaching emphasis: Wild produce is food, not dependable planting stock. Bumblezone cultivars provide a seed line that can be replanted after mature uprooting. Find Bumblezone planting stock and establish a farm at home.

### 11. Fruit Fuels a Sprint (`fruit_fuels_a_sprint`)

Trigger: You ate your first fruit.

Composition: Bright fruit arranged beside a winding trail and a gradually brightening amber motion ribbon; no runner or body parts.

Teaching emphasis: A varied diet matters: strong fruit nutrition supports sustained sprinting. Pack fruit when you expect a long journey.

### 12. Vegetables Buffer Exposure (`vegetables_buffer_exposure`)

Trigger: You ate your first vegetable.

Composition: Root vegetables in a sheltered stone alcove, with cold and heat weather on either side and a stable warm center.

Teaching emphasis: Strong vegetable nutrition helps protect against temperature swings and knockback. Keep vegetables in your travel food mix.

### 13. Dry Food for the Journey (`dry_food_for_journey`)

Trigger: You picked up food that can be dried.

Composition: Fresh food beside a simple drying rack and compact dried provisions packed for travel; no written labels.

Teaching emphasis: Dryable food can be prepared for travel before spoilage or cold makes it awkward to use. Check its drying recipe in EMI and prepare a reserve.

### 21. Ore Chunks Need Separation (`ore_chunks_need_separation`)

Trigger: You extracted your first geological ore chunk.

Composition: Jagged ore-bearing rock above a simple taut string mesh; a few separated mineral grains caught below, with no person or text.

Teaching emphasis: A chunk is raw deposit material; a String Mesh separates the useful ore before later machines become worthwhile. Build a String Mesh and process the chunk.

### 22. Maintain a Tinkers Tool (`maintain_tinkers_tool`)

Trigger: Your assembled Tinkers tool is running low.

Composition: A chipped Tinkers pickaxe beside matching repair material and a quiet workshop bench, with the restored edge suggested in reflection.

Teaching emphasis: A worn tool can be repaired and kept in service instead of rebuilt from scratch. Inspect its repair material and restore it before the next expedition.

### 23. Alloy in the Smeltery (`alloy_in_smeltery`)

Trigger: You used a Melter or Smeltery.

Composition: Small Tinkers melter beside a larger smeltery, two distinct molten streams blending into a single alloy casting.

Teaching emphasis: Tinkers molten metals can combine into alloys in this equipment; Create mixing serves other recipes. Check the available alloy recipe and pour a useful casting.

### 25. A Lit Furnace Produces Pollution (`lit_furnace_pollutes`)

Trigger: You lit a furnace.

Composition: Lit stone furnace sending a visible smoke stream toward a chimney flue, with dark air above an unvented outlet; no people.

Teaching emphasis: A burning furnace releases pollution. A chimney routes the exhaust; it does not erase the emission. Place and route a chimney before running a larger furnace bank.

### 29. Machine Blocks Open Root Machines (`machine_blocks_open_roots`)

Trigger: You inspected a Millstone or Mechanical Press recipe.

Composition: Millstone and Mechanical Press sketched as physical machines around a central sturdy machine block, with a material trail leading in.

Teaching emphasis: These early Create machines turn hand work into repeatable processing when rotational power reaches them. Hover over either machine in the Thread and use EMI to inspect its recipe. Build one, then give it rotation.

### 30. Plan Sustained Rotation (`plan_sustained_rotation`)

Trigger: You placed your first Millstone or Mechanical Press.

Composition: Hand crank driving a single early machine; a faint longer axle leads toward distant waterwheel and windmill beyond a copper Font.

Teaching emphasis: A Hand Crank can run the machine now. Sustained water or wind power becomes available after Nether access. Crank the first operation, then plan a later continuous drive.

### 37. Ratlantis Starts Logistics (`ratlantis_starts_logistics`)

Trigger: You inspected the Courier Lattice or scalable pipe route.

Composition: Simple hand-carried crate transitioning into branching pipes and a Courier Lattice, with dry Ratlantis island stone in the distance.

Teaching emphasis: Ratlantis is the next source for logistics equipment as automation outgrows hand delivery. Plan a Ratlantis trip and gather what the route requires.

### 38. Spirits Are Materials Too (`spirits_are_materials`)

Trigger: You acquired your first spirit.

Composition: Small luminous spirit wisps beside a Malum reagent vessel and a trader-scale set of copper trade weights; no figure or text.

Teaching emphasis: Spirits are both Malum crafting materials and a currency accepted by some traders. Keep a reserve and inspect both its crafting uses and trade offers.

### 43. Find a Dimensional Font (`find_dimensional_font`)

Trigger: Your first Tinkers tool is assembled.

Composition: Freshly assembled Tinkers pick in foreground, a folded cartographic map pointing across a barren landscape toward a distant radial copper Font.

Teaching emphasis: Dimensional Fonts provide the route out of the Overworld. Traders can sell maps that lead to them. Ask traders about Font maps and prepare for your first departure.

### 44. A Font Trip Has a Time Budget (`font_trip_time_budget`)

Trigger: You approached a Dimensional Font.

Composition: Radial copper Font in a stone court with a finite amber charge vessel and a clear return path winding home; no portal rectangle.

Teaching emphasis: A Font journey spends charge and has a return window. It helps to prepare supplies and a destination before entering. Check the available charge, pack essentials, and plan your return.

### 46. Vanilla Boats Break Into Parts (`boats_break_into_parts`)

Trigger: You boarded a vanilla boat.

Composition: A plain timber boat crossing rough water, one loosened plank and a compact pile of salvage at shore; no passengers.

Teaching emphasis: Boats in this pack have limited durability; when one breaks, some construction material comes back. Keep replacement material handy before a long crossing.

### 48. Ratlantis Continues Below the Islands (`ratlantis_below_islands`)

Trigger: You arrived in Ratlantis.

Composition: Dry Ratlantis island above a deep blue underwater cutaway with distant submerged ruins and a clear descending route.

Teaching emphasis: The dry islands are a foothold. More of the dimension lies below the water, beyond the first landing. Establish supplies on land, then prepare for a deep dive.
