## Primary gameplay promise in this source

The shipped *source implementation*, not the old authoring images or future plans, presents a Threads journal of discovered gameplay rules and an immediately available Lessons library. `src/main/resources/data/better_discovery_guides/threads/catalogue.json`, `ThreadDefinitions`, and `ThreadArt` define the 48 live cards, their committed-outcome routes and explanations; `ThreadSignals` credits the owner on the server only when a valid correlated outcome matches, persists discovery/unread/history/pending notices, and syncs the client. `ThreadPlayerState` scopes state by server and player UUID, with a Better World Management lineage backend when loaded and overworld SavedData fallback otherwise. `LoadingBriefs` loads 18 packaged lessons and filters optional-provider lessons by mod availability. `ThreadDeckScreen`, `LearningLibraryScreen`, `ThreadClient`, `ThreadNetwork`, and `ProviderHooks` provide the reader, previews, networking, and optional adapter routes. A lesson is **not** gated behind a discovery; a card is not awarded by merely reading a lesson. `ThreadFacsimileItem` describes its legacy copies as display-only, not unlocks or rewards.
